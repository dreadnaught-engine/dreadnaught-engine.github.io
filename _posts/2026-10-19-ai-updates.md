# Google Gemini 4.0

Been a while since my last post but I've been waiting on the update to Gemini before continuing with major design. I've been Gemini 4.0 (Argon) go through all my documents I've made in the past, and it is SO much better. The issue is that I can only do about 3 prompts every 4 hours, but I've been busy with schoolwork anyway. 

Which is a great thing, personally. I can spend about 15 minutes with the engine, processing what I need to process with AI, then go about my school stuff. Argon is actually vastly superior to the past Gemini Pro model - I can get better quality results and cross-check much more documents at once. I've made so much progress in the last 5 days or so, and I'm confident with the coming results.

Recently, I've been thinking about how I could do the visual and audio stuff. I know it's a ways out, and I shouldn't even bother until I get to that point, but there are some ideas I wish I could just draw or note down to save for a future date. 

The biggest one is music. I think of melodies all the time, but I have no means to record them. I guess I can hum them and save them somewhere, but humming really doesn't do it justice, and I hate the sound of my own voice. I've been thinking of buying a piano I can put in my keyboard drawer, and then learn a tiny bit of piano along the way. I just need to find a piano that's like no thicker than 2 inches, and as close to full-size as possible.
